## About my work

I design and investigate verifiable, evidence-bound approaches to **AI agent identity and authorization**, **scientific data integrity**, and **reproducible protocol testing**. My focus is on explicit trust boundaries: what a system can claim, what evidence supports the claim, who has authority to act, and how a verifier can detect a failed or incomplete transition.

I publish technical proposals and research artifacts under **Deonté Watts** (also shown as **Deonte Watts** and **Deonté O'dell Watts**) and maintain links to the original sources so readers can check status, versions, and provenance independently.

> **Research standard:** A proposal is not a proof. I distinguish design ideas, test results, independent verification, and externally adopted standards rather than presenting them as equivalent.
